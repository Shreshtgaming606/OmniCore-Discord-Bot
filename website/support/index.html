Common questions

Why does a moderation command fail?

Check both your own Discord permissions and the bot's permissions. The bot's highest role also needs to be above a target member or role where Discord requires it.

Why does the AI assistant not reply?

The bot team must approve the server for AI use. The assistant responds to mentions and replies in approved servers, and the configured Ollama service must be available.

How can I remove stored AI history?

A server administrator can run /delete_memory to remove that server's AI history without removing settings or lock records. You can run /delete_my_data to remove AI turns linked to your own account.

Request data deletion

For a fuller user or server deletion request beyond /delete_my_data and /delete_memory, open a privacy request through the issue link. Do not include your Discord ID, message text, or other private details in a public issue. Ask the team to arrange private verification.

We review requests against the data the bot stores and remove matching records after verifying account or server authority.
